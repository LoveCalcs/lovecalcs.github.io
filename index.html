A weekly relationship check-in: ten simple questions

Busy weeks have a way of filling every conversation with logistics: who is picking up the shopping, when the bill is due, what time the train leaves. A short weekly check-in gives a couple twenty minutes that are about the two of you instead. It is not therapy and it does not need to be serious. It is just a habit of asking how things are going before small things become big ones.

How to do it

Pick a regular time when neither of you is tired or rushed, such as Sunday morning coffee. Put phones away. Take turns answering, and let the other person finish before you respond. If a question leads somewhere difficult, it is fine to note it and come back to it another day.

The ten questions

  1. What was the best moment of your week?
  2. When did you feel most connected to me this week?
  3. Was there a moment you felt unheard or left out?
  4. What is one thing I did that you appreciated?
  5. Is anything stressing you out that I might not know about?
  6. How are we doing on sharing the jobs around the home?
  7. Is there anything we keep meaning to talk about and keep putting off?
  8. What would make next week feel a bit easier for you?
  9. Is there something fun we could plan, even something small?
  10. How can I show you I care in a way that really lands for you?

Keep it light

The point is to stay in touch with each other, not to keep score. Thank each other for answering honestly. Some weeks the check-in will take five minutes and end in a joke, and that is a good week.
