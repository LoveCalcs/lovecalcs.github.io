How to read a love calculator score

You type two names, press a button and get a number: 47%, 83%, maybe a perfect 100%. It is one of the oldest games on the internet, and it still gets people laughing, comparing and arguing in group chats. So what does the number actually mean?

Where the number comes from

A name love calculator does not know anything about the two people. It turns the letters of the names into a number using a fixed formula and shows the result as a percentage from 0 to 100. On the LoveCalcs love calculator there is no randomness: the same two names always give the same score, on any phone and in any of the 11 languages. Swapping which name goes first can change the result, because the formula reads the names in order.

What a high or low score means

Honestly? Nothing about your relationship. A 23% does not mean you are a bad match, and a 98% is not a promise. The score is a bit of fun, the same way a fortune cookie is. Couples who have been together for decades get low scores all the time, and that is half the joke.

Ways to make it fun

  • Try nicknames. Full names, first names and pet names all give different scores. See which version of you two scores highest.
  • Swap the order. Since the order can change the number, settle who goes first with a coin toss.
  • Guess first. Each of you writes down a guess before pressing the button. Closest guess picks the next film.
  • Use it to start a real conversation. Laugh at the number, then ask each other what actually makes you a good team.
